A network for what you can do next

Naxovelis is building a network that connects people, skills, ideas, projects and opportunities. We start in Nigeria, with students, graduates, freelancers and digital creators.

We are at an early stage and we are building this with the community, so what we offer will grow with what members need. We will always say clearly what exists today and what is still being built.

Meet the founder

Ojelugwu Gift Chinwe

Founder of Naxovelis · Graphic Designer & Tech Student

I am a graphic designer and tech student from Nigeria. I started Naxovelis because capable people often miss opportunities, not for lack of talent, but because they hear about them too late or cannot tell which ones are genuine.

Naxovelis is my answer: one network where reviewed opportunities, useful people and honest information come together. My design background shapes how I work. I want everything we build to be clear, simple and trustworthy, from the way we write to the way we check what we share.

We are at the very beginning, and I am building it step by step with the community. I will not promise more than we can deliver, and I will always tell you what is ready and what is still on the way. If you are a student, graduate, freelancer or creator, I would like to hear what would help you most.

Questions

What is Naxovelis?

An early-stage network that connects people, skills and opportunities, starting in Nigeria.

Who can join?

Students, graduates, freelancers, digital creators, and anyone looking for a way forward. Businesses and organisations can also get in touch.

Does it cost anything to join?

No. Joining is free.

How do I know an opportunity is real?

We review each one before sharing it, and we will leave out any we cannot verify. Still, always check the official source before you apply. Never pay anyone to get an opportunity, and tell us if someone asks you to.

How can I submit an opportunity?

Email naxovelis.network@gmail.com with the title, who it is for, the deadline and an official link.

Is Naxovelis available outside Nigeria?

We are starting in Nigeria. We hope to grow beyond it, and we will say so here when that happens.
